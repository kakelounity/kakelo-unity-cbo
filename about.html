About Us

Empowering communities through unity, opportunity, and sustainable development.

Kakelo Unity CBO is a registered community-based organization dedicated to improving lives through youth empowerment, education support, entrepreneurship, and community outreach programs in Kakelo Location, Rachuonyo East Sub-County, Homabay County.

Our mission is to create inclusive opportunities that strengthen families, uplift vulnerable groups, and inspire lasting social change in our community.

Our Mission

To empower communities by providing sustainable programs that foster education, leadership, economic growth, and social wellbeing.

Our Vision

A thriving and united community where every individual has equal opportunity to succeed and contribute positively.
