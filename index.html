Who We Are

Kakelo Unity CBO is a community-driven organization registered in Rachuonyo East Sub-County (Homabay County), committed to empowering individuals and transforming lives through inclusive and sustainable programs.

We focus on five core pillars: youth development, economic empowerment, education and mentorship, community service, and leadership & governance.

Through collaboration, innovation, and collective action, we foster resilience, self-reliance, and shared prosperity — building a stronger, more united community for today and tomorrow.
